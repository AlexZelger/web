The Imposter

Everyone shares one secret word — everyone except the imposter, who's flying blind. Drop one-word clues, sniff out the faker, and vote. Best played in the same room or on a call.

How it plays

  1. Everyone gets the same secret word from a shown category — except one random imposter, who only sees the category.
  2. Going around in order, each player gives one word hinting at the secret. The imposter has to bluff something that fits.
  3. Everyone votes on who the imposter is.
  4. Caught? The imposter gets one shot to guess the word and steal the win. Slip away uncaught and they win outright.
